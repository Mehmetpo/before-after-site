About Before & After Pro

⚡ A fast, free, and private image comparison tool — built for photographers, designers, and anyone who loves visual storytelling.

Why We Built This

We believe comparing images should be simple, fast, and respect your privacy. Most online tools require uploads to servers, accounts, or subscriptions. Before & After Pro works entirely in your browser — nothing leaves your device.

Features

  • 4 comparison modes — Slider, Side-by-Side, Fade, and Onion Skin
  • Editing tools — Zoom, pan, rotate, brightness, and contrast adjustments
  • Multiple exports — Download combined, individual, or snapshot images
  • Dark & Light themes — Easy on the eyes, day or night
  • Keyboard shortcuts — Power users can fly through comparisons
  • Fullscreen mode — Distraction-free viewing
  • 100% client-side — Zero server uploads, zero tracking, zero accounts

Tech

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — zero dependencies, zero frameworks. Glassmorphism UI with animated backgrounds, smooth transitions, and responsive design.
